Study options

Test: pick a choice on the front (the choices are shuffled each time). A wrong pick, or I don’t know, leaves only Again; a right pick leaves all four buttons with Good suggested.

0 means no review limit, and no leech rule. A leech is a card you keep failing: after that many lapses it is suspended and listed under Stats. Fuzz nudges long intervals by a day or so so cards graded together do not all return together; the interval shown on a button can differ from the real one by that much.

Defaults (20 new a day, 90% target recall, learning steps 1 and 10 minutes) are Anki’s defaults for its FSRS scheduler. Scheduling is done by the open-source FSRS library ts-fsrs 5.4.2 with its published default weights unless you paste your own below. Weights are applied to future grades only.

          The CSV has one line per grade (card_id, review_time, review_rating, review_state, review_duration) so a tool that fits FSRS weights to your history can read it. Fitting needs a few hundred reviews at least.
